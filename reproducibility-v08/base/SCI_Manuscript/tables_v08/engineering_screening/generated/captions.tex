\newcommand{\EngineeringScalarCaption}{%
Secondary engineering-proxy error differences: capacity-matched DeepSets minus sum GNN; positive values favor the GNN. All differences and interval endpoints are in $10^{-3}$ response units. End-time error uses the 60-min response; $J$ is the trapezoidal time-average of the 61-point trajectory. Equal-case errors are averaged within each seed and then across five seeds. Intervals use the existing 1,000 crossed geometry--seed resamples and are unadjusted secondary intervals. $N$ counts geometries, not repeated predictions; populations overlap across protocols. These are archival-response proxies, not physical capacity or fire-resistance validation.}

\newcommand{\EngineeringScreeningCaption}{%
Discrete archival-response screening at $q=0.8$ and $0.6$. $E/C$ gives reference events/right-censored cases by 60 min, each geometry counted once. Events use the first stored value at or below $q$ on the 60-s grid. Each model reports the separate maximum FN and FP counts over five seeds and its minimum reference--prediction common-event coverage $n_{\mathrm{com}}/E$. These extrema may come from different seeds and are not one worst checkpoint. Common events are model-specific, not a shared two-model cohort. Coverage is NA when $E=0$; undefined rates and conditional times remain NA in the all-model CSVs. Zero observed errors do not establish zero future risk.}
