Composition of the 997-case archival benchmark. (a) Exact case counts across ten prescribed-fire families and crack counts $N=1$--15; row totals are shown in parentheses. Zero-count cells are retained. The dashed line marks the graph-size holdout boundary. (b) Contributions from the two source batches. For the crack-count study, 733 cases with $N=1$--8 form the training and validation population, and 264 cases with $N=9$--15 form the test population. All high-count cases originate from source batch~005, so count transfer is accompanied by a source-batch shift. (c) Retaining 86 constant-response cases from the same archive restores smoldering coverage from 11 to 97 cases; the population comprises 911 previously retained cases plus these 86 restored cases. Bar widths show composition, not predictive performance or confidence intervals. These are archived scalar-response records, with the target interpretation and numerical limitations stated in the text.
