\textbf{GNN-selected engineering response examples from complete fire-family holdouts.}
The four distinct cases are selected from the 997-case population, with each case evaluated only under its own completely held-out fire-family protocol. Panels a--c show the cases at zero-based ranks 498, 946 and 996 of the sum-GNN case MAE averaged over five seeds; ties use the declared lexicographic rule. Panel d shows the remaining case with the largest five-seed mean of the maximum positive prediction-minus-reference error. If the remaining maximum is zero, that fact is explicitly retained. These are GNN-conditioned descriptive examples, not a representative sample of matched-DeepSets tails or a new model-advantage test.
The dark reference and actual seed42 predictions are shown on the 61 stored evaluation times. Each model's shading spans its observed pointwise minimum and maximum over seeds 42--46. These ranges are not confidence or prediction intervals, and cases from different fire families may use different independently trained checkpoints. The aligned temperature strips reproduce the recorded prescribed exposure functions; they do not establish realized finite-element boundary amplitudes. Selection scores and the displayed seed42 MAE and positive peaks are distinguished and reported in percentage points of the dimensionless archived response.
Dashed guides mark thresholds 0.8 and 0.6. Crosses indicate the first stored time with response at or below the threshold, using each displayed trajectory separately. No event time is interpolated, extrema bands are not treated as trained trajectories, and recrossings are retained. Absence of a marker denotes right censoring beyond 3600 s; an event at the horizon is marked only when a stored value actually meets the threshold. Error direction refers to the archival response, not a verified structural safety limit.
