\textbf{Prespecified IID comparison on the 997-case archival benchmark.}
\textbf{a}, Equal-case mean absolute error (MAE) on the same 160 test cases for seven models, each independently trained using five matched random seeds; hollow circles show seed-level values and dark ticks show their arithmetic means. Models follow the frozen protocol order.
\textbf{b}, The two prespecified paired MAE contrasts. Diamonds indicate mean differences, with positive values favoring the second named model. Lines show percentile 95\% intervals from the existing 5,000-resample bootstrap that resamples geometry clusters and training seeds. These approximate intervals describe paired case and seed variability; five seeds provide limited information about the seed distribution. Time points are not resampled.
\textbf{c}, Empirical cumulative distributions across test cases of the MAE for each case averaged over five seeds. These are distributions of seed-mean errors, not errors of ensemble-averaged predictions; the dashed horizontal line marks 95\% of cases.
All errors refer to the dimensionless archived scalar-envelope target and are expressed in percentage points (100 times the source MAE). This figure contains all 35 completed and independently audited IID runs and does not establish transfer performance or physical-capacity validation.
