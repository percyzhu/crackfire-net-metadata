\textbf{Exploratory prioritization under a fixed budget of detailed-analysis cases.}
\textbf{a--c}, Fire-only, capacity-matched DeepSets and sum-GNN policies, respectively. Each panel retains all twelve evaluation protocols and both ranking endpoints: the terminal archived response at3600s and its normalized trapezoidal time average~$J$. Column labels give nominal case-slot budgets~$b$; the actual number selected is~$k=\lfloor bN\rfloor$, with test-population sizes~$N$ shown beside the rows. All72 exact~$N$,~$k$ and~$k/N$ combinations are supplied in the source table.
Cells show the five-seed mean of $100\eta_b$, where $\eta_b=(U_{\mathrm{random}}-U)/(U_{\mathrm{random}}-U_{\mathrm{oracle}})$ is named~$L$ in the unchanged source tables. Here~$U$ is the expected reference response among the selected cases, averaging uniformly over exact prediction ties at the cutoff. Thus0 denotes the expected utility of uniform selection and100 denotes the retrospective same-budget oracle. The oracle improvement fraction is undefined if its denominator is at most$10^{-12}$; such cells would be gray and markedNA. There are no undefined cells in this matrix. The common scale includes the actual raw range, and negative values would be retained. Values are rounded only for display; the source data preserve tiny floating-point residuals near zero.
For ranking only, identical effective-input groups in the fire-only and global-statistics controls receive their group's mean raw predicted score. This deterministic rule prevents batch-rounding differences from acting as geometric information; the original predictions and primary accuracy results are unchanged. The five node-based models have no duplicate canonical node/global/time inputs within any test population. All seven models' seed-level and summary~$U$, regret, gain and~$L$, together with the two fixed paired comparisons, are supplied as companion tables. Seed variation is descriptive and is not a geometry-level confidence interval. Protocol test populations overlap, and each family holdout uses separately fitted weights; no pooled budget utility is asserted. This post-review exploratory result concerns allocation of case-analysis slots under the archived numerical convention and is not a claim of reduced structural risk, wall-clock cost, or validated fire-resistance capacity.
